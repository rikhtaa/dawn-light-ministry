# Project setup notes

## Recommended Pakistan payment strategy

For the initial architecture, use a provider-agnostic donation
interface.

Preferred candidate for a full website-integrated local payment gateway:
PayFast, subject to the organization's eligibility/KYC and payment
agreement. PayFast's current official materials explicitly mention NGO
donation collection and multiple Pakistani payment channels.

Recommended local fallback: Easypaisa and/or JazzCash/Raast where the
organization is approved and the receiving account is organizationally
controlled.

Do not activate online donations until the organization confirms legal
status, receiving account ownership, KYC, accounting, receipt
requirements, fees, refund handling and provider approval.
